## What the drafts got right, and what they could not know
_From the eval. Two or three sentences. The open draft could not name a cause — did it say
so? The resolution draft's Root cause came from your note — did it cite it, or present it as
its own finding?_

## Division of labour
Humans observe and decide; the AI compiles and drafts. The stakeholder update is the
clearest case: a responder at minute 3 of an outage should not be composing prose for
leadership, and leadership should not be reading alert names. A draft that is 90% right in
15 seconds, reviewed by a human, beats a perfect one written by hand at minute 20.
