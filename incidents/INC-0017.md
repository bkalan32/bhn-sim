# INC-0017 — Settlement reconciles zero records; the job refuses to call it success (Day 14, game day 1, fault 2)

Three minutes after fault 1, and unrelated to it, the settlement CronJob's env was set to
`SETTLEMENT_FAIL_MODE=silent`. Since Day 8 the job self-checks: zero records → exit 2, not
"settlement complete". So the quiet failure was loud — two alerts from two signals, a
ticket, an automatic re-run that failed for the same reason and said so, and a bounded
retry that succeeded once a human had fixed the cause. The second incident of the
afternoon, and the one the scenario expected the responder to miss.

## What happened

The env change did nothing until the next cron tick. At 14:50 the job started, found zero
records, and did what Day 8 taught it: pushed `records=0`, did **not** touch
`last_success`, logged `refusing to report success`, and exited 2. Two rules saw it from
two directions — `SettlementZeroRecords` on the pushgateway metric first, `SettlementJobFailed`
on kube-state-metrics two minutes later — so the "quiet" fault could not have been quiet.

The responder was elsewhere: reading the egift ticket, then checking why the *Remediator
scraped* stat had gone red. That check produced a pod list, and the pod list had an `Error`
in it. From there the log named the cause in one line. The overview would have shown the
settlement row red by about 14:53; nobody looked at it again after 14:50:54. That is the
finding: fault 2 was found by luck of adjacency, not by the dashboard built to show it.

The remediator's story is the good one. Its re-run job was created 47 seconds before the
human's fix and failed for the original reason — and the FAILED note quoted that reason
onto the ticket, which is a diagnosis. It then waited its 180 seconds, and its single retry
ran against the fixed CronJob and succeeded. The ticket shows human and automation in
order: policy, failure with cause, human fix, automatic recovery.

## What went well / badly

Well: detected twice, from two signals; the Day 8 self-check turned a silent failure into
a loud one for the second time; the remediator followed the policy to the letter and its
notes carried the cause; the retry finished the recovery.
Badly: noticed from a pod list, not the overview — the responder never returned to it;
the tier-3 note (`ZeroRecords`) followed two minutes later by an AUTO action (`JobFailed`)
reads as a contradiction; 15 of the 18 minutes on the ticket were the alert's window.
