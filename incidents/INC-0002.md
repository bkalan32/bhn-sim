## Symptom
eGift p95 order latency climbed from ~0.2s to ~1s. Order rate unchanged, error rate unchanged.

## What the dashboard said
"Slow." Nothing else. Same curve as INC-0003.

## What the trace said
`activation POST /activate` was the widest span by far (~800ms of a ~900ms order).
`generate_code` and `send_email` were normal.

Trace ID: _paste_

## Cause
Activation dependency degraded (simulated: `BASE_LATENCY_MS=800`).

## Who gets paged
**The activation team.** eGift did nothing wrong; it is waiting on a dependency.
Paging the eGift on-call for this wastes a person and delays the fix.

## Follow-up
- Should eGift have its own alert on `egift_step_latency_seconds{step="activate"}`, so
  it can say *"my dependency is slow"* rather than just *"I am slow"*?

  against it?
