## What happened

At 20:42:07Z half of egift's orders started failing at the email step. `EgiftHighErrorRate`
fired 2 m 49 s later; the ticket opened with three sources and, 33 s after that, a hypothesis
that named kb-003 at high confidence, explained the five reasons, dismissed the activation
cascade and quoted the team's prior answer — escalate, tier 3. The remediator declined and
said so. The responder, busy reverting fault 1 and waiting out fault 2, reached it seven
minutes after it opened, posted the partner ticket as a note, "recovered the partner" by
resetting the rate, and the platform closed it two minutes later.

## What went well / badly

Well: the best hypothesis the bot has written — the KB did exactly the second job it was built
for (name the look-alike, dismiss it on its documented discriminator), and the decisive
evidence was a CloudWatch histogram. Badly: seven minutes from ticket to first human touch
while two other incidents were open — a *critical* that waited behind a *warning*, because
the warning was already in hand; a second responder would have taken this one at 20:45.
Also: the closing brief reported this incident's error rate as "3.4 %" — the rate at report
time, not the incident's — and never said "escalated".
