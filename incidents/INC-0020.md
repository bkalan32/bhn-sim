## What happened

At 20:33:06Z the scenario set `BASE_LATENCY_MS=600` on activation, which rolled the pods.
Every request completed and every request was slow. `ActivationLatencyBudgetBurn` fired
3 m 35 s later as a warning; the bot opened `INC-1789245416-72a4` fifteen seconds after that
with three collectors attached and a hypothesis that named the wrong dependency at medium
confidence for a reason the responder could see through in one line. The copilot, asked the
question the PDF prescribes, went to the deployment itself and found the env var and the
8-minute-old pods. The change was reverted at 20:47:50Z; the alert cleared five minutes later
and the platform closed the ticket at 20:52:56Z. Nothing automated acted, correctly.

## What went well / badly

Well: the *warning* routing held (a ticket, no page); tier 3 was right and said why; three
sources on a cloud ticket; the copilot's trail was real from first hand to last, including a
logs search that ran on CloudWatch through a translated query; the revert was reasoned, not
reflexive. Badly: the hypothesis' lead was baseline noise; the deploys collector reported
"nothing" for a change that rolled every pod — the third incident where a hand change was
invisible to the enrichment (0015's drift, 0016's `kubectl set env`, now this) — and the
closing brief repeated "Deploys: none in 24h" after two real rollouts.
