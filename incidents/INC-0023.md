# INC-0023 — Fraud dependency outage, handled in the browser (Day 22 drill)

**The first incident handled without a terminal.** From the approval that broke production to
the approval that fixed it, every observation, note, grade and decision happened in Mission
Control's browser UI (mission-control:55). The terminal was used before the clock (to queue both
halves of the fault) and after it (to recover from a PC restart); never during the incident.

Fault: `FRAUD_SVC_DOWN=true` on activation — kb-001, the platform's oldest known failure
(INC-0001). Tier 3 by the team's own policy: no safe automated action; escalate to the provider.

## What happened

The two halves of the fault were queued from the terminal at 22:24:24Z and the terminal was
left alone. At 22:25:06Z the fault was approved from the banner — the tier-2 ceremony Day 21
built, now a button. The Overview went red within a minute (activation 0.0, egift 30, platform
43), the feed showed `ActivationHighErrorRate firing` at 22:27:43Z and both tickets opened
twelve seconds apart, cause first. The incident page carried the whole Day 9/10/12/17 stack: the
context card (every number linked to its PromQL), a medium-confidence hypothesis citing kb-001,
and — because kb-001 is tier 3 — the grey **"No safe automated action: escalate"** card with the
team's fix text instead of a button. The responder read both hypotheses, posted the escalation
and the cascade note, graded the diagnosis, and approved the "provider recovered" change at
22:40:02Z.

## Durations: why the tickets say 228 minutes

Nothing in the audit log or the timelines between 22:40:02Z and 02:41:25Z — the lab was not
running (the PC went down and was restarted; the platform's first events afterwards are at 02:16Z). The alerts could not resolve on a
platform that was not evaluating them; when it came back at 02:16Z, Prometheus and Alertmanager
started without their alert state, both tickets were closed as resolved, and the slow-burn
`ActivationLatencyBudgetBurn` (6 h window, still full of the drill's latency) re-fired and opened
**INC-1790216186-4a5a** — an artefact, noted as such. The KPI table must use the impact window
(≈15 min) for this incident, not `duration_min`. A ticket's duration is when the platform *saw*
it end; when the platform is not running, that is not when it ended.

## What went well

- **Every step of the ceremony was a click, with a name.** Both tier-2 approvals are audit rows
  with `entrance=button`, operator `bkalan32` and the token — the Slack button Day 12 described.
- **The hypotheses held up with a source missing.** The logs collector returned "no events"
  while activation failed ~7 requests/s. Neither hypothesis invented a histogram; both said the
  missing data contradicted kb-001's signature, lowered confidence to **medium**, and kept the
  diagnosis on the evidence that agreed (latency capped at the fail-fast timeout, no deploy).
  That is the Day 10 rule doing its job on a real gap, not a staged one.
- **The cascade was called correctly.** The egift hypothesis did what the Day 14 and Day 19 game
  days needed a human for: it pointed away from the service that was shouting, using the step
  latency — the discriminator kb-003 documents.
- **Tier 3 looked like tier 3.** No button to press, the fix text in the team's words, the
  escalation as a note.

## What went badly

- **The log pipeline had been down for five hours and nothing said so.** The first real use of
  the incident page found it: after the power cut, Docker restarted the containers in a different
  order and Splunk moved from .3 to .4; Fluent Bit kept sending to .3, then dropped every chunk it
  could not deliver. A second restart then showed HEC itself had silently gone back to HTTPS.
  Fixed at the root (CORRECTIONS-REBUILD **B10** pinned addresses, **B11** HEC scheme asked, not
  remembered). The part still open: no alert fired. `FluentBitNotShipping` is a Day 24 item.
- **Mission Control's incident tile timed out** (`incident-bot: ReadTimeout`) in the minute the
  tickets opened — the bot is busiest exactly when the screen matters most. The tile degraded
  alone, as designed, and recovered on the next poll; worth watching on game day 3.
- **Drafts rendered as raw markdown** (`##`, `**`) — fixed during the drill (CORRECTIONS-DAY22 N5).
