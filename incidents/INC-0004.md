## What was different about this alert
Day 3's alert said *"error rate above 10%."* This one said *"whole month gone in ~2
days."* Same underlying fault. One is a number; the other is a business consequence
with a deadline. Which would you rather read at 3 AM, and which would you rather
forward to your director?

## The two windows, observed
- **5m burn rate** — jumped to ~100× within a minute of injection
- **1h burn rate** — climbed slowly; crossed 14.4× after ~_N_ minutes
- After rollback the **5m** dropped below 14.4× in ~_N_ minutes and the alert resolved,
  while the **1h** was still reading _N_× — the short window is what lets it resolve

## Follow-up
- The service runs at **4× burn permanently** at its 2% baseline. It will never meet
  99.5%. Is the SLO wrong, or is the service? (Answer: the 2% is simulated
