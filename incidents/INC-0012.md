# INC-0012 — Tier-1 drill: a crash-looping pod, restarted by automation (Day 12)

A throwaway Deployment (`crashtest`) whose container exits at startup. `PaymentsPodCrashLooping`
fired, the bot opened a ticket, and the remediator deleted the pod — without asking, because a
Deployment replaces a deleted pod and the action is strictly reversible. Then the replacement
crash-looped too, and the remediator said so.

Four tickets, three refusals, zero wrong actions. Every refusal named its reason on the record.

## What tier 1 is for, and what it is not
A restart fixes transient problems and merely masks persistent ones. Here it masked nothing:
the failure was in the image, so the replacement failed identically. The value was the
*sentence* — "restart did not stick, this is real" on the ticket within two minutes, plus the
still-firing alert — which is exactly the signal a human needs to skip the restart step and
look at the code. A naive self-healer would have kept deleting; the cooldown stopped the second
delete. This is the classic critique of naive self-healing, and now you have seen it.
