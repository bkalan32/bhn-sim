did not move, write the Splunk `stats count by app.reason` search, read the answer. Today the
record had the same three answers within a minute of the ticket, before a human looked, and
the diagnosis was right — the human's job became *confirming* it, not finding it.

## What it got wrong, and why it matters

bridge summary the hypothesis is usable (zero invented numbers or events); as a runbook it is
not. Queued for Day 11's prompt: a platform-facts block (namespace `payments`, the four
service names, only metrics present in `context.metrics`, the four Splunk fields). Day 11's
copilot *runs* the checks, which removes the failure class — a tool call cannot invent a namespace.
