## Time to detect, time to close — the two lags worth understanding

- **Fault → first alert:** _N_ s. That is `for: 2m` on `ActivationHighErrorRate` plus one
  evaluation interval. Detection has been at this speed since Day 3.
- **First alert → ticket:** _N_ s. That is `group_wait` (15s) plus the webhook. Grouping costs
  seconds and saves duplicate tickets — a deliberate trade.
- **Recovery → ticket closed:** _N_ min. Not slowness: the 2m and 5m alert windows have to
  drain, then `group_interval` (2m) batches the resolved notification. An incident closes when
  the *signals* clear, not when the fix lands. Real ticketing systems behave the same way, and
  new on-call engineers routinely mistake this lag for a broken integration.
